# ✨ Learnable

### Make learning feel like progress.

Learnable turns big learning goals into small, satisfying steps. Choose a topic, build a habit, and watch your progress grow—one session at a time.

> **Learn a little. Grow a lot.**

---

## 🌱 What is Learnable?

A learning app concept built to make personal growth feel clear, encouraging, and achievable. Whether you're exploring a new subject or developing a skill, Learnable helps you keep moving forward.

## 🚀 The experience

- **Pick a goal** that inspires you.
- **Break it into bite-sized steps** that fit your day.
- **Build a learning streak** with consistent practice.
- **Celebrate milestones** and see how far you've come.

## 🧭 How it works

1. Choose what you want to learn.
2. Set a goal that feels achievable.
3. Complete a focused learning session.
4. Track your progress and take the next step.

## 💡 Our vision

Learning should feel less like a mountain and more like a path—with small wins along the way. Learnable is here to help turn curiosity into lasting progress.
